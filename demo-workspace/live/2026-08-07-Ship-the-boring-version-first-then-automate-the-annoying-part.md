## Key Ideas

* Automate the step you dread, not the step that is easy
* A checklist is a valid v1

## Why It Matters

* Premature automation locks in a bad process

## Recommendations

1. Run the workflow by hand five times
2. Automate the single worst step

## Transcript

I keep seeing people automate step one of a ten step process. Do it by hand first. The step you start avoiding is the one worth automating. Everything else can stay a checklist.
