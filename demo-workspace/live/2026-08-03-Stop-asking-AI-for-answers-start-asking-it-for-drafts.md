## Key Ideas

* Editing a draft is faster than validating an answer
* Drafts expose the model's assumptions

## Why It Matters

* Answer-mode hides reasoning, draft-mode shows it

## Recommendations

1. Reframe prompts as draft requests
2. Keep final judgment human

## Transcript

When you ask for an answer you get confidence. When you ask for a draft you get something you can push against. I only ask for drafts now. My edit is the quality gate.
