## Key Ideas

* Capture friction kills more ideas than bad ideas do
* Transcription plus tagging makes voice searchable

## Why It Matters

* An idea you never wrote down has zero value

## Recommendations

1. Set up a one-tap voice capture route
2. Auto-transcribe into a reviewable inbox

## Transcript

You lose the idea in the time it takes to open a notes app and pick a folder. Voice is instant. Let the machine transcribe and file it. Review once a day, keep or kill.
