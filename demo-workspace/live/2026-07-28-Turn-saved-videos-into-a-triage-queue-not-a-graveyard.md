## Key Ideas

* Every capture needs a next action: review, task, or dismiss

## Why It Matters

* Ten thousand bookmarks and zero actions is the default outcome

## Recommendations

1. Add statuses to captures
2. Review weekly, promote the best to tasks

## Transcript

Your saved folder is where ideas go to die. Mine used to be too. Now every save lands in a queue with a status. Once a week I promote the good ones into actual tasks and dismiss the rest.
