## Key Ideas

* One variable per experiment
* Keep a lab notebook for prompts

## Why It Matters

* Unlogged experiments get repeated forever

## Recommendations

1. Log hypothesis, change, and outcome
2. Review the notebook before new experiments

## Transcript

I wasted a month changing three things at once and learning nothing. Now every experiment gets a line in a log: what I changed, what happened. Boring? Yes. Repeatable? Also yes.
