## Key Ideas

* Index parked work so parts are findable
* Reuse beats restart

## Why It Matters

* Unindexed work is a write-off, indexed work is an asset

## Recommendations

1. Write a one-page index of parked projects
2. Harvest components before starting anything new

## Transcript

I stopped feeling bad about unfinished projects when I started treating them like a parts shelf. Half my new builds start with something I already wrote. The index is what makes that possible.
