## Key Ideas

* The review is where systems get repaired

## Why It Matters

* Tools drift without a maintenance loop

## Recommendations

1. Block thirty minutes weekly
2. Review queues, statuses, and one metric

## Transcript

Every system I run degrades during the week. The weekly review is the repair shop. Thirty minutes, same time, non-negotiable. Skip it twice and the whole machine is lying to you.
