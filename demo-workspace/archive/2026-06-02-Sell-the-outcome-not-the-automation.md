## Key Ideas

* Lead with the metric that moves
* The plumbing is your problem, not the pitch

## Why It Matters

* Feature-first pitches lose non-technical buyers

## Recommendations

1. Quantify one outcome per offer
2. Keep architecture out of the first call

## Transcript

Nobody wakes up wanting an automation. They want the Friday afternoon back. Sell the Friday afternoon. Show the wiring only if they ask.
